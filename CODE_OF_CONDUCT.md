# Code of conduct

ROAI Purser is built by people who want AI agents to be safe and accountable. We expect the
same care in how we treat each other.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Accept a decision once it is made, even if you argued for something else.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or comments about anyone's identity or body.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Where this applies

This code applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere
someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Email **admin@roai.us**. We read every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and
reply within 5 business days.

## What happens next

The maintainer decides what to do. That can be a private warning, removing comments, or a
temporary or permanent ban from the project.
