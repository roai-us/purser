# Licensing

ROAI Purser is free to use, and its source code is public. It is source available
rather than open source, because its license limits one kind of commercial use.

| Part                | License                                         |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `packages/roi-core` | [Apache License 2.0](packages/roi-core/LICENSE) |
| Everything else     | [Elastic License 2.0](LICENSE)                  |

This page is a plain summary. The license files are what count.

## What you can do for free

- Use ROAI Purser, including at your company and for commercial work.
- Run it on your own computers or in your own cloud, for your own organization.
- Change it, and share copies of it, as long as you pass on the license, keep the
  copyright notices, and clearly mark any copy you changed.

## What needs a commercial license

Offering ROAI Purser to other people or companies as a hosted or managed service, where
they get access to a substantial set of its features.

To ask about a commercial license, write to [admin@roai.us](mailto:admin@roai.us).

The Elastic License 2.0 has two other limits: you can't remove or hide licensing,
copyright or other notices, and you can't get around any license key checks.
