- **"Term"** means the Initial Term in the Order Form and every renewal under Section 7.

## 8. Termination

**8.1 For breach.** Either party may end the Agreement by written notice if the other party
materially breaches it and does not fix the breach within 30 days after receiving written
notice describing it. For unpaid fees, the period is 10 days.

**8.2 For insolvency.** Either party may end the Agreement by written notice if the other
party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or
becomes the subject of a bankruptcy or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within
60 days.

**8.3 Wind-down.** When the Agreement ends for any reason other than termination by
Licensor under Section 8.1 or 8.2, Customer may keep operating each Licensed Service for
its existing End Customers for up to 90 days (the "Wind-down Period"), at no extra charge,
to move them to another solution. Customer may not add new End Customers during the
Wind-down Period.

**8.4 Refund.** If Customer ends the Agreement under Section 8.1 because of Licensor's
breach, Licensor will refund the fees Customer prepaid for the part of the Term remaining
after the termination date.

**8.5 Effect.** After the Agreement ends, and after any Wind-down Period, the license in
Section 3 ends and Customer will stop providing the Software as a hosted or managed
service. Customer keeps whatever rights it has under the Public License. Sections 4.5 (for
the final report), 5 (for amounts owed), 8.4, 8.5 and 9 to 16 survive the end of the
Agreement.
